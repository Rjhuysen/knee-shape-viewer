About the model

Shape = population mean + CVA CAA−1(xA − μA): ordinary least squares from the ticked measurements A to all 310 SSM modes (femur + tibia, 4,798 vertices), fitted on 305 cases. The app contains only this OLS relationship (mean shape, measurement–vertex and measurement–measurement covariances); the SSM itself (modes, eigenvalues, per-case data) is not included.

Hold-out error (notebook Measurements2SSM_Flexion): mean shape ≈ 16 mm; FL + Flexion + HKA + TL ≈ 5.3 mm; all 27 ≈ 3.3 mm point-to-point RMSE.

Flexion is computed on the meshes (shaft axes in the sagittal plane), not taken from the clinical table.

Moving one slider while many correlated measurements stay fixed can give implausible shapes. Watch the plausibility indicator.
